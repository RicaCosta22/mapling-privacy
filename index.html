Mapling privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Mapling: Guess the Country ("Mapling") is a daily geography game for Android, published on Google Play by RC Dev Studio. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Mapling collects no personal data and shares none. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your phone

To remember your games, Mapling saves your guesses and results for each daily puzzle in the app's own storage on your phone. Your stats and streak are worked out from those saved games. This information never leaves your phone and is never sent to RC Dev Studio or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share, Mapling passes a short text (the puzzle number, coloured squares for your guesses and a link to the app on Google Play) to Android's share menu. You choose where it goes. Mapling doesn't see or keep what happens next.

Links

The About screen opens this policy in your browser and the contact address in your email app. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Google Play

Google provides app downloads and updates, and Android may send Google crash and performance reports if you allow it on your phone. Google's own privacy policy covers that. Mapling adds no reporting of its own.

Children

Mapling is not directed at children under 13. It collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
